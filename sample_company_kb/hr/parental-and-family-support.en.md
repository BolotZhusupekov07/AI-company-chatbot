# Parental And Family Support

Ala-Too Digital supports employees with family-care responsibilities. Employees should discuss upcoming needs with their manager and People Operations as early as practical.

## School And Kindergarten Adaptation

Parents may request flexible start times for up to two weeks when a child starts kindergarten or school. The manager should approve the request unless it conflicts with urgent production or customer commitments.

## Family Emergency

Employees may request emergency leave for urgent family situations. People Operations records the leave type after the employee is safe and able to provide details.

## Nursing And Care Breaks

Employees who need care-related breaks should agree on a practical schedule with their manager. The arrangement should protect privacy and avoid unnecessary public explanation.

## Documentation

People Operations may ask for supporting documents only when required for payroll, compliance, or extended leave processing.
