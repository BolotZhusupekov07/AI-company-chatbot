# Confidential Salary Review Notes

This document is restricted to the HR group. It is included in the sample knowledge base so permission filtering can be tested later.

## Salary Review Cycle

Salary calibration happens twice per year, in April and October. HR prepares a recommendation pack for leadership based on market data, performance review results, internal equity, and budget limits.

## Bishkek Market Notes

HR tracks local software-market signals for Bishkek and remote Central Asia roles. The benchmark set includes backend engineers, QA automation engineers, product managers, HR operations specialists, and customer success managers.

## Confidentiality

Salary bands, individual compensation notes, and leadership calibration comments must not be shared with employees outside the HR and leadership review process.

## Exception Review

Off-cycle salary exceptions require written justification from the department lead and approval from HR and Finance. Common reasons include role expansion, retention risk, and correction of a documented pay-equity issue.
