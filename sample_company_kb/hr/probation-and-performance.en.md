# Probation And Performance Review

The standard probation period is three months. The manager and employee agree on probation goals during the first two weeks and record them in Ak-Bata.

## Probation Checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Timing | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goal setting | First 2 weeks | Manager |
| Midpoint feedback | Week 6 | Manager and employee |
| Final review | Week 12 | Manager and HR |

## Regular Reviews

Performance reviews happen twice per year. The review focuses on delivery outcomes, collaboration, ownership, learning, and support for teammates.

## Review Inputs

Managers should include:

- delivery examples
- customer or stakeholder feedback
- code review and quality notes for technical roles
- mentoring or knowledge-sharing examples
- attendance and reliability patterns when relevant

## Improvement Plan

If performance concerns are identified, the manager and HR may create a written improvement plan with measurable goals and dates.

Improvement plans are confidential and visible only to the employee, manager, HR, and the responsible department lead.
