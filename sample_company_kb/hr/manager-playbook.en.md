# People Manager Playbook

This document is for people managers and HR. It explains how managers should handle recurring employee situations consistently.

## One-On-Ones

Managers should hold one-on-ones at least every two weeks. Notes should focus on goals, blockers, wellbeing, growth, and decisions. Private employee details should not be copied into public project tools.

## Probation Risks

If a manager sees probation risk before week 6, they must contact HR before giving a final warning. HR helps confirm whether expectations were clear, feedback was specific, and support was offered.

## Burnout Signals

Managers should watch for repeated late-night work, sudden communication drop, missed recovery days, unusually high irritability, and frequent context switching. The first response should be workload review, not blame.

## Escalation

Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, safety issues, and salary confidentiality breaches must be escalated to HR immediately.
