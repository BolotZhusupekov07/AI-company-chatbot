# Benefits And Wellbeing

Ala-Too Digital provides a benefits package designed for a Bishkek-based hybrid software team.

## Standard Benefits

| Benefit | Limit | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Learning budget | 35,000 KGS per year | Courses, books, conferences, and certification exams |
| Mobile internet support | 1,200 KGS per month | For employees with approved remote days |
| Sports support | 2,000 KGS per month | Gym, swimming pool, yoga, or team sports |
| Mental wellbeing sessions | 6 sessions per year | Confidential external provider |

## Health And Burnout

Employees who feel overloaded should speak with their manager or People Operations before the situation becomes urgent. Managers may reduce meeting load, pause non-critical work, or approve recovery time.

## Team Events

Company events usually happen once per quarter. Events should be inclusive for employees who do not drink alcohol, employees observing religious practices, and employees with family-care responsibilities.
