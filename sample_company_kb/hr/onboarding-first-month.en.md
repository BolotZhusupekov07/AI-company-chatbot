# First Month Onboarding

Every new employee follows the Ak-Bata onboarding checklist during the first month. The checklist is owned by People Operations, but the manager is responsible for role-specific tasks.

## Day One

Day one is usually held at the Ak-Keme Hub in Bishkek. The employee receives a laptop, office badge, Tunduk Desk account, payroll forms, and a short introduction to company policies.

## First Week

During the first week, the employee should complete:

- account setup
- security basics
- team introduction
- benefits overview
- probation goals draft
- first one-on-one with the manager

## First Month

By the end of the first month, the employee should have a confirmed probation plan, a named buddy, and access to all role-specific systems.

## Buddy Program

Each new hire gets a buddy from a different project when possible. The buddy helps with company habits, office navigation, meeting norms, and informal questions.
