# Confidential Employee Relations Notes

This document is restricted to HR. It exists in the sample knowledge base to test that sensitive HR content is excluded from normal employee retrieval.

## Sensitive Case Categories

Employee relations cases include harassment reports, retaliation concerns, repeated conduct issues, safety complaints, and conflicts involving managers.

## Handling Rules

HR records only necessary facts, dates, involved parties, actions taken, and follow-up commitments. Speculation and personal opinions should not be stored in case notes.

## Access

Access is limited to HR. People managers may receive outcome instructions, but they do not receive full HR case notes unless HR explicitly approves it.

## Retention

Employee relations notes are retained according to the internal retention schedule and are never exported into general search tools.
