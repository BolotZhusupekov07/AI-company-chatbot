# Vacation Policy

Full-time employees receive 24 paid vacation days per calendar year. Vacation days accrue monthly from the employee's start date.

## Request Process

Vacation requests are submitted in Tunduk Desk at least 10 working days before the planned start date. Managers approve requests based on team coverage, client delivery windows, payroll deadlines, and support responsibilities.

For urgent family travel, employees may request an exception through People Operations. The exception must include the dates, reason category, and manager confirmation.

## Carryover

Unused vacation days may be carried into the next calendar year up to the limits below.

| Unused Days At Year End | Carryover Limit | Expiration |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 1-10 days | All unused days | March 31 |
| 11 or more days | 10 days | March 31 |

## Restrictions

Employees should not plan vacation during a scheduled production launch, payroll export week, or a customer go-live week unless the delivery manager approves an exception.

## Local Travel Note

For vacation inside Kyrgyzstan, employees are encouraged to set a backup contact if they will be offline in areas with unstable mobile internet, such as mountain routes or remote lakeside areas.
