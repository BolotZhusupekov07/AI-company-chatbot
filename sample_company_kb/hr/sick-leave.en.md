# Sick Leave Policy

Employees should notify their manager as soon as they know they cannot work due to illness. A short private message to the manager is enough for the first day.

## Medical Certificate

A medical certificate is required when sick leave lasts more than three consecutive working days.

## Paid Sick Leave

Paid sick leave is available to all full-time employees after the probation period ends.

## Care Day

Employees may use up to two care days per quarter to support a sick child, parent, spouse, or close family member. Care days are tracked separately from vacation days.

## Confidentiality

Managers may ask for the expected return date, but they should not ask employees to share private medical details in public channels.

## Remote Recovery

If an employee is well enough to work but cannot commute safely, the manager may approve temporary remote work for up to three working days.
