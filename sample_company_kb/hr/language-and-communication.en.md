# Language And Communication Policy

Ala-Too Digital works in a multilingual environment. English is the default language for durable documentation, while Kyrgyz and Russian may be used for live collaboration.

## Documentation

Long-lived documents should be written in English so they are searchable and reusable across teams. HR may add Russian summaries for high-impact policy changes.

## Meetings

Meeting owners should choose the language that lets all required participants contribute. If a decision affects multiple teams, the decision summary must be written in English after the meeting.

## Employee Support

Employees may contact People Operations in English, Russian, or Kyrgyz. Sensitive HR conversations should happen in the language the employee is most comfortable using.

## Customer Context

Customer-facing employees should follow the language preference agreed with the customer. Internal notes about customer requests should still be summarized in English.
