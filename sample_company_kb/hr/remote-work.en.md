# Remote Work Policy

Ala-Too Digital supports hybrid work for most roles. Employees should agree on remote days with their manager and keep their calendar accurate.

## Working From Another City

Employees may work from another city in Kyrgyzstan for up to 15 working days per quarter with manager approval. Common examples are Osh, Karakol, Naryn, Jalal-Abad, and Cholpon-Ata.

The employee must confirm stable internet, private workspace, and ability to join meetings during Bishkek core collaboration hours.

## Working From Another Country

Working from another country requires approval from the manager, HR, and Finance before travel. This is needed because tax, payroll, data access, and client contract rules can apply.

## Availability

Remote employees must be reachable during core collaboration hours from 10:00 to 16:00 Bishkek time.

## Border And Travel Constraint

Employees should not take company laptops across a border for work without written approval. Personal travel with a company laptop is allowed only when the employee has approved remote-work-from-abroad status.
