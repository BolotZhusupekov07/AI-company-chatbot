# Laptop And Equipment Policy

Ala-Too Digital provides a company laptop for full-time employees. Additional equipment may be requested if it is needed for the role.

## Standard Equipment

| Role Type | Standard Equipment |
| --- | --- |
| Engineer | Laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse |
| Designer | Laptop, monitor, drawing tablet if approved |
| Operations | Laptop, headset |

## Care

Employees are responsible for keeping company equipment secure and in good condition. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT and HR immediately.

## Return

All equipment must be returned on the final working day unless HR approves another date.
