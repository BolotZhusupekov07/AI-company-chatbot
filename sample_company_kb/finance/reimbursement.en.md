# Expense Reimbursement

Employees may request reimbursement for approved business expenses. Requests must include a receipt, business purpose, amount, currency, and manager approval.

## Common Reimbursable Expenses

- business travel within Kyrgyzstan
- approved training courses
- work-related software subscriptions
- office supplies approved by the manager
- mobile internet package used during approved regional remote work
- taxi from the office after 21:00 when late work was approved by a manager

## Deadlines

Expense requests should be submitted within 30 calendar days of the purchase date.

## Currency

Finance records reimbursements in Kyrgyz som. Expenses in another currency are converted using the National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic exchange rate for the purchase date.

## Receipt Rules

Receipts may be submitted in Kyrgyz, Russian, or English. If the receipt does not clearly show the business purpose, the employee must add a short explanation in the reimbursement request.
