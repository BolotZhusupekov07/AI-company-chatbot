# Payroll Calendar

Payroll is processed monthly. Salaries are usually paid on the last working day of the month.

## Timesheets

Contractors and hourly employees must submit timesheets by the 25th day of each month. Late timesheets may move payment to the next payroll cycle.

## Bank Details

Employees must send bank detail changes to Finance at least five working days before payroll processing.

## Payslips

Payslips are sent by email after payroll is completed.

## Local Bank Notes

Employees may receive salary through a Kyrgyzstan bank account in KGS. Finance validates account details before the first payroll and after any bank change request.

## Payroll Freeze

Finance applies a payroll freeze two working days before payment. After the freeze starts, changes to bank details, unpaid leave, or contractor hours move to the next correction cycle unless Finance approves an urgent fix.
