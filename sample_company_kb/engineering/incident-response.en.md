# Incident Response

An incident is any production issue that affects customer operations, data integrity, security, or service availability.

## Severity Levels

| Severity | Description | Initial Response Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEV1 | Major outage or data-loss risk | 15 minutes |
| SEV2 | Partial outage or serious degradation | 30 minutes |
| SEV3 | Minor production issue with workaround | 1 business day |

## Communication

The incident lead posts updates every 30 minutes for SEV1 and every 60 minutes for SEV2 until the issue is mitigated.

## Postmortem

SEV1 and SEV2 incidents require a postmortem within five working days.
