# Working Hours And Public Holidays

The standard working week is Monday to Friday. The default schedule is 09:00 to 18:00 Bishkek time, with a one-hour lunch break.

## Flexible Start

Employees may start between 08:00 and 10:00 if their team calendar allows it. Core collaboration hours are 10:30 to 16:30 Bishkek time.

People Operations asks managers not to schedule recurring meetings before 10:30 unless the meeting includes a customer in another time zone.

## Kyrgyzstan Public Holidays

Ala-Too Digital follows official public holidays in Kyrgyzstan. HR publishes the final holiday calendar each December for the next year.

The holiday calendar usually includes dates such as Nooruz, Orozo Ait, Kurman Ait, Independence Day, and New Year holidays. Exact dates are taken from the official government calendar once announced.

## Time Zone

All internal deadlines use Bishkek time unless the document explicitly states another time zone.

## Short Fridays

From June through August, teams may use short Fridays when delivery commitments allow it. Employees may leave after 16:00 on Friday if their manager confirms there are no urgent releases, interviews, support escalations, or payroll deadlines.
