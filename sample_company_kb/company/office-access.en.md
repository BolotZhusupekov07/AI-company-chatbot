# Office Access

Employees can enter the Ak-Keme Hub from 08:00 to 22:00 on working days. The reception desk is staffed from 09:00 to 18:00 Bishkek time.

## Badges

New employees receive an access badge during the first office onboarding session. Access badges are personal and must not be shared, even with teammates.

Lost badges must be reported to People Operations and the office coordinator on the same day. The badge is disabled immediately and a temporary badge can be issued for up to three working days.

## Office Zones

| Zone | Purpose | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Issyk-Kul Hall | Training, onboarding, all-hands | Employees and registered guests |
| Son-Kul Room | HR interviews and confidential conversations | HR and invited employees |
| Arslanbob Focus Area | Quiet engineering work | Employees |
| Archive Cabinet | Printed HR and finance records | HR and Finance only |

## Guests

Guests must be registered at least one business day before the visit. The host employee is responsible for meeting the guest at reception and staying with them in non-public office areas.

## Emergency Contact

For office access problems outside business hours, employees should call the office coordinator listed in Janylyk.
