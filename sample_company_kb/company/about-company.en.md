# About Ala-Too Digital

Ala-Too Digital is a fictional software company based in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan. The company builds HR operations software, internal employee-service portals, payroll integrations, and workflow automation tools for customers in Central Asia.

## Office

The main office is called the Ak-Keme Hub. It is located in central Bishkek and is designed for hybrid teams: focus rooms for engineering work, small rooms for HR interviews, and one training room used for onboarding sessions.

## Working Language

Company-wide documents are written in English first. HR announcements are also summarized in Russian. Kyrgyz may be used in office communication, internal events, and employee-support conversations when it helps the employee communicate clearly.

## Teams

| Team | Main Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| People Operations | Onboarding, employee support, benefits, and internal policy |
| Payroll Integrations | Salary file exports, bank-format integrations, and payroll reconciliation |
| Employee Portal | Self-service HR workflows and employee-facing web features |
| Platform | Backend services, infrastructure, and internal developer tooling |
| Customer Success | Support for HR teams using Ala-Too Digital products |
